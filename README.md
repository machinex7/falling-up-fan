# falling-up-fan
Some ideas for an app for Falling Up fans, just to help play around in the universe, learn more of the lyrics and songs and trivia, while trying to also have that little mystique of the world.

# Space Exploration Game
Take inspiration from sci-fi UI’s. Like https://mir-s3-cdn-cf.behance.net/project_modules/1400_opt_1/171ec987499903.5db9e4eb6646d.jpg\
The App loads into a sci-fi cockpit type of view. Lots of buttons and dials and widgets.\
[Album List] is a large button on the right side. [Search Songs/Lyrics] [Band Members] \
You should be able to rank tracks/albums in your favorite order, and Star albums, songs, or lyrical sections.\
[Launch] is on the bottom. Click it for all of the widgets to power on, and the central panel to open, showing a view of the stars. Flip switches and hit blinking lights to launch.\
Ideas: Power Redirection/Collection. Lyric Search. Stellar Cartography. Stellar Navigation. Repair Drone Deployment. Material Synthesis. Asteroid Mining. Sweeping Stardust. Dismiss/Fix Warnings. Chemical Analysis. Engine Calibration. Log Discrepancy Review. Exotic Waveform Collapse.\
Story: You are a traveler across the stars, carrying a precious payload to a planet far, far away. I’d like Jessy to write parts of the story.

**Mechanics**


**Events**
Struck by an asteroid, throwing you a little off course but causing lots of damage.\
Turn on the game after a while to find yourself on a crash course for a planet/sun.\
Black Hole. Little warnings that you can ignore, but they rapidly build in intensity as you are pulled in.\
Worm Hole. Pops up with no warning. Teleports you elsewhere.

**Encounters**
Ice Field. Move carefully through it, but you can mine lots of H2O.\
Asteroid Field. Move carefully through it, but you can mine lots of minerals.\
Supernova Remnants. Pick up highly radioactive metals for the power system.\

Puzzle Ideas
- Lyrics on the left. Tap words to have symbols appear. You have to use <=3 of those symbols to solve a puzzle on the right side.
- The lyrics are like keys. They have to be inserted in the right place.
- Places on the renders have a few glowing circles. These are locations to visit. You can moev between them and collect and use items.
- Drag the lyrics under a scanner, or a scanner over the lyrics, to reveal secrets.
 
It may be hard to make interesting puzzles out of all the lyrics. I don't like letter/word puzzles. I like meaning and content puzzles. So not puzzles where the puzzle is creating new words and the like.
